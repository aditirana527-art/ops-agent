Title: Procurement and Replenishment Policy (v1)
Effective: 2023-04-01
Status: superseded by v2 on 2025-07-01

## Reorder trigger
Reorder a product when its stock_on_hand falls to or below its reorder_level. Order the product's reorder_qty from its default supplier.

## Purchase order approval
Purchase orders with an estimated cost above 5,000 USD require procurement manager sign-off. Purchase orders at or below 5,000 USD may be raised directly by operations staff.

## Supplier selection
Always use the product's default supplier.
