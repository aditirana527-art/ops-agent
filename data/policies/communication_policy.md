Title: Communication and Escalation Guidelines
Effective: 2025-01-15
Status: active

## Tone
Customer messages must be professional and concise, apologise for delays, and never reveal internal costs, supplier names or credit details.

## Internal teams
Valid internal recipient ids are: procurement, finance, warehouse, sales-ops, management.

## When to escalate to a human
Use a human review flag whenever: data conflicts cannot be resolved by policy; a required action falls outside policy; there is a supply risk with no alternative supplier; or a decision carries financial exposure above 2,500 USD without an approval path.

## Notification content
Include order and PO ids and concrete dates. Do not include speculation. Keep each notification to a single topic.
